# Services for TDTU Academic and Professional Staff

## Essential Guides
Academic and professional staff can access guidance on Library Card & Account information, library orientation, intellectual-property and copyright rules, and FAQs.

## Borrowing Materials
Available services include borrowing library materials and interlibrary loan.

## Materials Purchase Requests
Staff can submit requests to purchase materials for general library use or for personal use.

## Teaching Support
Teaching-related support includes course reserves, information-supply services, subject guides, and customized library tutorials for students.

## Research Support
Research support includes dedicated research services, research guides, research tools, and guidance for using TDTU Library links in Google Scholar.

## Other Services
Additional services include instruction and information-literacy programs, library spaces and facilities, Studio access, IT support, new-arrival information, and library-visit registration.
