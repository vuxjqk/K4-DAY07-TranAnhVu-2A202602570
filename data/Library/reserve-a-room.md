# Reserve a Room

## Booking and Cancellation
Library rooms are booked through the Library's room-booking service. To cancel, users may contact the Library by phone, email, Facebook, or speak with staff at a Service Desk/Information Desk.

## Usage Time
- Group Study, Presentation, Video, and Individual Study Rooms: up to 2 hours per session.
- Studio, Online Conference Room, and Moonlight Area: booking time depends on user needs.

## Extensions
For Group Study, Presentation, Video, and Individual Study Rooms, an extension is possible when no reservation is waiting.

## Fees
Room-specific charges are shown in the booking interface under Room Details.

## Students Using Temporary Cards
Students who completed library training can book online with their Student Information Portal account. Students using temporary paper cards should ask library staff for help accessing the booked room.

## Capacity Rule
The registered group must reach at least 50% of the room's capacity and may not exceed the room's maximum capacity.
