# Library Card & Account

## Library Card
At TDTU, the library card is the user's university student, lecturer, or staff card.

### Undergraduate Students
Library-card support is handled by the Department of Student Affairs, Room A0003, Building A. Contact: (028) 37 755 054; studentaffairs@tdtu.edu.vn.

### Postgraduate Students
Support is handled by the Department of Graduate Studies, Room B002, Building B. Contact: (028) 37 755 059; gradstudies@tdtu.edu.vn.

### Lecturers and Staff
Support is available from INSPiRE Library, Building G. Contact: (028) 37 755 057; library@tdtu.edu.vn.

## Library Portal Account
The Library Portal uses the same credentials as the Student Information Portal or Lecturer/Staff Information Portal.

### Undergraduate Students
Students without a portal account should contact the Student Services Office, Room E0001, Building E: (028) 2247.7215; tuvanhocduong@tdtu.edu.vn.

### Lecturers and Staff
Users who already have a staff portal account but lack Library Portal access should contact INSPiRE Library. Users without a staff portal account should contact the Department for Personnel & Administrative Affairs.
