# Services for TDTU Undergraduate Students

## Essential Guides
Undergraduate students can access guidance on:
- Library Card & Account information.
- Library Orientation sessions.
- Vietnam intellectual-property law and copyright.
- Frequently Asked Questions.

## Borrowing Materials
Services include:
- borrowing TDTU Library materials;
- interlibrary loan.

## Materials Purchase Requests
Students can request materials for general library use or for personal use.

## Learning Support
Support includes:
- course readings;
- subject guides;
- required reading lists by course.

## Research Support
Students can use research-support services, research guides, and research tools.

## Other Services
Additional services include instruction and information-literacy programs, spaces and facilities, computer support, new-arrival information, and guidance on copyright compliance and effective library use.
