# Undergraduate Student - Circulation Service

## Loan Periods

### Non-circulating Materials
- Maximum: 2 items.
- Loan period: 1 day, within library opening hours.
- Renewal: not available.

### Circulating Materials
- Maximum: 2 items.
- Loan period: 5 days.
- Renewal: one additional 5-day period.

## Renewal
Items can be renewed only while they are on loan, are not overdue, and have not been requested by another user. Renewal is completed online through the Library Information Portal.

## Fines and Lost Items
- Overdue fine: 20,000 VND per day per item.
- Repeated violations can trigger a library warning and, after more than three violations in a year, referral for further handling.
- Lost-item compensation depends on whether the item is still available, out of print, part of a set, or rare.

## Material Reservation
Users may place a hold on materials currently checked out by another borrower. After receiving a campus-email pickup notice, the reserved item is collected at the first-floor Service Desk and is held for 1 day.

## Borrower's Responsibilities
Borrowers must care for materials, keep their library card private, report a lost card, monitor due dates, and return materials early if the Library issues a recall.
