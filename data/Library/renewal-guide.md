# Renew Library Materials

Renewal extends the borrowing period of checked-out materials. The number of additional days depends on the circulation policy for the relevant user group.

## Ways to Renew
- Use the Library Portal renewal function.
- Contact the Library by phone at (083) 775 5057.
- Contact the Library by email at library@tdtu.edu.vn.

## Online Renewal
The Library provides separate online renewal guidance for undergraduate/postgraduate students and for lecturers, researchers, and staff.

## When Renewal Is Not Allowed
Renewal is not available when:
- the material is already overdue; or
- another user has placed a hold request on the material.
